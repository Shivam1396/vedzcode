Good food, without the wait

MIT Canteen serves freshly prepared meals to students and staff every day. Our smart ordering system lets you choose your meal online and pick it up when it is ready.

Our story

Lunch breaks are short, and long queues at the counter take up most of them. We built the Smart Canteen so that students spend their break eating, not standing in line.

Our kitchen team cooks breakfast, lunch and dinner fresh every day. You can see what is on the menu, place an order from your phone or laptop, and follow it until it is ready to collect.

Meals and timings

The canteen is open for three meals a day.

Meal Time What we serve
Breakfast 7:30 AM – 10:30 AM Poha, idli sambar, masala dosa, tea and coffee
Lunch 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM Veg thali, paneer masala, veg biryani
Dinner 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM Thali, snacks and cold drinks

How ordering works

Four steps, from sign up to your first bite.

  1. Create an account

    Sign up once with your student ID.

  2. Choose your meal

    Browse the menu and add dishes to your cart.

  3. Pay online

    Place your order and pay in a few taps.

  4. Collect it

    Track your order and pick it up when it is ready.
