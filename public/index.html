About us

MIT Canteen serves hundreds of students every day, and the rush at meal times means long queues. This app lets you order ahead, pay by UPI QR, and collect your food using your token number. The canteen team sees every order live, so food is ready faster and nothing gets missed.

1. Login

Create a free account with your email.

2. Order & pay

Add food to the cart and scan the QR to pay.

3. Collect

Show your receipt token at the counter.
